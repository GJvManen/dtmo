## RC13.3 identity boundary

DTMO validates externally issued production bearer tokens. The RC13.3 managed principal/role registry is auditable provisioning/assignment state and does not mint, forge or silently rewrite active bearer-token claims. Production role changes require identity-provider reconciliation or token reissue.

Built-in security roles remain code-controlled. Service accounts remain restricted to the `service_account` role; browser administration cannot create arbitrary custom token roles or combine machine and human/admin authority.

## Security and governance

- server-side RBAC remains authoritative;
- source execution, analytics and Administration access do not grant publication authority;
- human review and external share approval remain distinct decisions;
- separation of duties is preserved;
- privileged RBAC mutations require a human admin plus `manage:users`;
- self-management and final-admin lockout protections apply;
- RBAC mutations are audit-recorded with request correlation;
- Grafana anonymous access remains disabled;
- provenance, privacy, auditability and fail-closed execution requirements remain in force.
