## Plan

Blocking gates:

- direct principal, token-resource and request identifiers may not appear in privacy projections;
- pseudonyms must be deterministic within a purpose and separated across purposes;
- retention expiry must be deterministic and timezone-safe;
- a documented legal hold must override ordinary expiry;
- production must require a dedicated pseudonymization secret;
- immutable source audit records and their cryptographic chain must remain unchanged.

- added explicit `keep`, `expire` and `legal_hold` dispositions;
- required a dedicated production pseudonymization secret of at least 32 characters;
- required identity projection retention not to exceed audit projection retention;
- added regression coverage for minimization, stable purpose separation, expiry, legal hold, timezone validation and weak secrets.
