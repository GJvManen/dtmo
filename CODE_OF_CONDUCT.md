# DTMO Code of Conduct

DTMO aims to maintain a professional, technically rigorous and inclusive collaboration environment.

## Expected behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on work, evidence and risk rather than people;
- welcome good-faith questions, review and correction;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, attribution and intellectual-property obligations;
- avoid publishing credentials, personal data, exploit details or sensitive operational information without authorization;
- disclose conflicts of interest that could materially affect a review or decision;
- respect separation of duties and never pressure reviewers or approvers to bypass security or release controls;
- use project systems only for lawful, authorized security work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate misinformation, unauthorized security testing, credential disclosure, abuse of project access, evasion of review controls and intentional weakening of security or evidence requirements are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Concerns should be raised privately with the repository owner or maintainers when public discussion could create privacy or security risk. Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, revoke access or escalate a matter when necessary to protect participants or the project.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented where appropriate and avoid disclosing confidential or personal information unnecessarily.

## Scope

This policy applies to DTMO repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, project-managed communication channels and conduct performed in an official project capacity.
